Since you're cleaning up a "giant mess" and need to archive and delete duplicates, here are your One-Click Command Tiles.

## 📂 Phase 1: Organizing & Archiving
Use these to move files into new or existing folders.


Create a folder named "2025 Archive" and move @[File Name] into it.


Move all @[File 1], @[File 2], and @[File 3] into the @[Folder Name] folder.


Organize the loose PDFs in my root directory into a new folder called "Unsorted Docs."


## 🧹 Phase 2: Finding Junk to Delete
Gemini cannot "empty" your trash or auto-delete folders yet, but it can hunt them down so you can bulk-delete them. [6, 7] 


Find every file with "Copy of" or "(1)" in the title and show them to me.


List all files larger than 100MB that I haven't opened in over 1 year.


Show me all folders that are currently empty.


## ⚡ The "Magic" Organize Button
If you are in Desktop Mode on Chrome Beta, look for this specific UI element directly in your file list (not in the chat). It is the fastest way to fix a mess:


* The "Organize files" Button: It usually sits at the top of your file list.
* What it does: It creates a "Suggested" tile. You click it, and it says: "I found 15 loose spreadsheets. Move them to a new 'Finance' folder?"
* One-Click Action: You just hit "Accept" or "Move files" and it does the whole batch at once. [6, 8] 


It is necessary to use the Desktop Web view to get these "agent" powers. [3, 9]
