Two things to report before we go further.

**Disk space kills the "clone all 121 in one shot" plan.** This device has only 15GB free (86% full already). GitHub's compressed size for your repos sums to ~20GB, but a real checked-out clone runs bigger than that — I compared the harness repo's GitHub-reported size (156MB) against its actual clone on disk (296MB), a ~1.9x inflation. Extrapolated across all 121 repos, a full clone stack would need roughly 35-40GB — over double what's available, and would risk filling the Android device's storage entirely, not just Termux's slice of it. That's not a "few times as possible" batch, that's not-happening-at-all as a single operation.

Workable alternative: shallow clones (`--depth 1`, no history) cut size dramatically, or we do genuinely small batches (10-15 repos at a time, checking free space between batches) and I delete/re-clone as needed rather than keeping all 121 checked out simultaneously. Either way, "one clone session for all 116" isn't safe on this hardware — which one do you want?
