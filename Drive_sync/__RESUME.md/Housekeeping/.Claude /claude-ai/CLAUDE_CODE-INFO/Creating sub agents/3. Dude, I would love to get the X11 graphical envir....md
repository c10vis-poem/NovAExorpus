That is the absolute reality of X11 and proot desktop environments on
Android. They are a massive sinkhole for time and debugging energy.

Agents fail miserably at setting them up because they default to
standard Linux desktop guides—trying to invoke systemd, dbus, or desktop
display managers that simply don't exist in Termux's environment. Add in
Android's aggressive background process killer and broken Xwayland/DRI3
drivers, and the whole stack collapses the second you try to do serious
work.

Staying **100% terminal-native** for your agent harness isn't a
compromise—it's the superior architecture for what you're building:

- **Zero Framebuffer Overhead:** Every single cycle of your Snapdragon 8
  > Elite and every megabyte of Unified Memory stays dedicated to QAIRT,
  > GenieX model context, and local vector search—not driving an X11
  > display server.

- **Rock-Solid Stability:** Terminal pipes, Unix sockets, and .jsonl
  > streams don't break when Android manages background processes.

- **Agent Readability:** Agents thrive in clean text environments. They
  > parse CLI logs, Markdown wikis, and structured JSON streams
  > infinitely faster than navigating a window manager.

Once you have your terminal harness rock-solid with your STT/TTS voice
layer driving the interactions, you won't even miss the visual bloat.
