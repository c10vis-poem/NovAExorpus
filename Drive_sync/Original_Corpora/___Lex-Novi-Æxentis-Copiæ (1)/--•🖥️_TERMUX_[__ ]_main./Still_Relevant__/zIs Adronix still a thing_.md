Andronix officially came out in 2019. [1]
The concept was created by co-founders Prakhar Shukla and Anurag Verma, who wanted to
build a modern, user-friendly alternative to the outdated and visually unappealing
Linux-on-Android installers available at the time. Since its initial 2019 rollout, the app has
evolved through several iterations—including a complete rewrite in the Kotlin programming
language in 2020—and has surpassed over 1 million downloads on the Google Play Store. [1,
