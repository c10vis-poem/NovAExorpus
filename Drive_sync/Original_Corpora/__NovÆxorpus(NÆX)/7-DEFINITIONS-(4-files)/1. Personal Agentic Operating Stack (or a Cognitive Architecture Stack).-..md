You are looking at the components of a **Personal Agentic Operating
Stack** (or a **Cognitive Architecture Stack**).

When you bundle Open Brain (OB1), mem0, LLM Wikis, and reasoning banks
together, you aren't just configuring "settings"—you are building a
**Cognitive Infrastructure**.

Here is how those specific pieces categorize under that umbrella:

## 1. The Core Infrastructure (The Backend & Protocol)

- **Nate B. Jones’s Open Brain (OB1) / Obi-Wan:** This is your **Unified
  > Memory Protocol & Persistence Layer**. It's the structural database
  > design (separating raw data from embeddings in SQL via MCP) that
  > ensures *any* AI you plug into it shares the exact same memory of
  > you.

- **mem0:** This acts as the **Dynamic Memory Management Layer**. While
  > Open Brain handles the structural storage sink, tools like mem0
  > manage the continuous extraction, updating, and pruning of user
  > preferences and facts on the fly.

## 2. The Context & Knowledge Layers (The Brain's Workspace)

- **LLM Wiki / Wiki MD Architecture:** This is your **Conceptual
  > Knowledge Graph**. It's the human-and-AI-readable markdown structure
  > (using internal linking) that replaces bloated RAG with clean,
  > traversable topical trees.

- **Reasoning Bank:** This is your **Cognitive Execution History /
  > Epistemic Ledger**. Unlike raw data, a reasoning bank stores *how*
  > problems were solved, successful multi-step logic paths, agent
  > trajectories, and decision-making templates so the agent doesn't
  > have to reinvent the wheel next time.

## Summary

If someone asks what you are building, you tell them you are spinning up
a **Local Agentic Cognitive Architecture** backed by an **Open Memory
Protocol (OB1)** and a **Structured Markdown/JSONL Knowledge Pipeline**.
