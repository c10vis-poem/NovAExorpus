=========================
Qualcomm VoiceAI ASR
=========================

Qualcomm VoiceAI ASR (automatic speech recognition) is a software development kit (SDK) for building AI based voice applications.
It offeres end to end solutions including multiple algorithms and notebooks, flexible integration on Qualcomm chipsets.

Contents
--------

- Notebooks for whisper-small, whisper-medium and whisper-large
- Whisper SDK for supporting whisper model running on multiple OS systems based on Qualcomm chipsets.
