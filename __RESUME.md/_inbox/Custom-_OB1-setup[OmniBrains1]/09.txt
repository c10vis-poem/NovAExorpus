## ReasoningBank (Also Public)

ReasoningBank — `google-research/reasoning-bank` — from Google Research (ICLR 2026 paper). It's a **memory mechanism for agents that learns from both successful and failed trajectories**. It distills reasoning strategies from interaction traces, stores them with semantic embeddings, retrieves relevant ones for new tasks, and feeds back into the bank. That's your recursive training loop.
